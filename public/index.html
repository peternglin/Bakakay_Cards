📖 How to Play Bakakay

  1. Create or join a room. Bakakay supports 2–10 players.
  2. When the host starts the game, the 52-card deck is automatically dealt among all players.
  3. The first player chooses one or more cards from their hand and plays them facedown.
  4. The player declares a rank and number. For example: “3 Kings.”
  5. The next player must decide: TRUTH or LIE.
  6. If they choose TRUTH, the claim is accepted and play continues using the same rank.
  7. If they call LIE, the cards are revealed. The game determines whether the declaration was truthful.
  8. A player who collects all four cards of a rank removes that four-of-a-kind from the game.
  9. The first player to have zero cards wins Bakakay!
💡 Tip: You don't have to tell the truth. Sometimes the best move is knowing when to bluff.
